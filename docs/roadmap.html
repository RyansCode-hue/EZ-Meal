Stage 2

Become EZ Plan

EZ Plan follows our four-part capture system. Meals become one area inside it; the planner, grocery list, and pantry carry over.

  1. CaptureGet anything out of your head in seconds, from any screen, into one shared inbox.
  2. OrganizeTurn each item into what it is: a task, a grocery item, a meal, a recipe, or a note.
  3. ReviewOnce a week, on Saturday's planning day, walk the inbox, the week, and open tasks together.
  4. EngageEach day, open to what matters now: tonight's dinner, what is due, and who is on it.

Open decisions

  1. Capture model Decided

    Capture, Organize, Review, Engage. Close to GTD, without a separate Clarify step; deciding what an item is happens during Organize.

  2. Is everything shared, or can each of you keep private tasks?

    Shared is simpler. Private tasks need a per-person filter on every list and reminder.

  3. Which lists to start with?

    Proposed: Home, Errands, Meals, Someday. Also whether a list can hold projects with their own steps.

  4. When should Android builds resume?

    Phase 9 assumes soon, once the website feels stable.

  5. When to buy the Apple Developer account?

    It gates the iPhone app in Phase 11 and voice capture on her phone.

  6. Should chat history be per phone or shared?

    Shared means either of you can pick up a conversation the other started.
